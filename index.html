Questions people ask

Who makes the films?

Two brothers who are just getting started. We use AI tools and our own editing, and we talk to every customer personally on WhatsApp.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the length and the story. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you the price before we start.

Will the people in the film look like us?

Your real photos are the base of every scene. AI adds motion, transitions, music and narration.

How many photos do I need?

Around 20 to 100 photos works well. Short videos and voice notes make it richer.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. We share a script and preview first, so you can tell us what to fix before the final film.

Is my data safe?

We only use your photos for your film, we do not share them, and we delete them when you ask.
